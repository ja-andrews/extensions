# CheckDomainRating.com for Raycast

Not sure if a website is worth a link, a pitch or a deal? See its rating out of 100 without leaving what you are doing.

The rating is an independent 0 to 100 logarithmic rating based on the number and authority of unique linking domains, computed from our own web-scale link index, refreshed monthly.

## Commands

- **Check Rating**: type a domain, or leave it blank to rate the site in your frontmost browser tab. Safari, Chrome, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi and Opera work straight away. Other browsers work with the Raycast browser extension.
- **Rate a List**: copy a list of domains or links, then run this. You get every rating in one list, and can copy them all as CSV. Up to 1,000 at a time.

## Setup

1. Get a free API key at [checkdomainrating.com](https://checkdomainrating.com/en/free-domain-rating-api-key).
2. Paste it into the extension's preferences the first time you run a command.

A free key covers 25 requests a day, and each request rates up to 100 domains. The limit resets at midnight UTC. A [plan](https://checkdomainrating.com/en/bulk-domain-rating-data) removes the daily limit.

## Privacy

Only the domains you check and your key are sent, to checkdomainrating.com. Nothing is stored by the extension, and we keep no record of the domains you look up.
